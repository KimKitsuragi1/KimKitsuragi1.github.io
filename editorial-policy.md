# Editorial Policy / 编辑声明

## 中文声明

《瑞瓦肖评论》是一份独立开放评论刊物，刊发法律、叙事、媒介、公共文化与后现代批判相关的工作论文、评论文章和实验性理论写作。

本刊不是同行评议学术期刊，不以正式学术评价为目的。所有文章均以开放工作论文形式发布，并可通过 Zenodo 等开放仓储生成 DOI，以便引用、存档与版本管理。

本刊强调以下原则：

1. **公开性**：文章应尽可能提供可核验来源、参考文献和版本记录。
2. **非伪装性**：本刊不伪装为正式学术期刊，不声称同行评议。
3. **可引用性**：文章可通过 DOI、版本号、发布日期和稳定链接进行引用。
4. **实验性**：本刊接受理论评论、文艺批判、概念论文、案例札记和风格化讨论。
5. **责任性**：涉及现实案件时，应区分事实、判决、报道、网络争议和作者解释。

## English Statement

Revachol Review is an independent open essay journal. It publishes working papers, essays, critical notes, and experimental theoretical writing on law, narrative, publicity, and public culture.

Revachol Review is not a peer-reviewed academic journal. All published works are editorially curated and openly archived. Each article may be deposited on Zenodo or other open repositories for DOI-based citation.

Core principles:

1. **Openness**: authors should provide verifiable sources, references, and version records when possible.
2. **Transparency**: the journal does not claim to be peer-reviewed.
3. **Citability**: articles should include DOI, version, date, and stable URL when available.
4. **Experimentality**: theoretical essays, cultural criticism, conceptual papers, case notes, and stylized discussions are welcome.
5. **Responsibility**: real cases should distinguish facts, judgments, reports, online controversies, and interpretation.
